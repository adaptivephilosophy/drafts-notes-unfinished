* Voice assistant UX — "hold on" / pause without buttons
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: voice-ux
:END:
Problem: When the user talks to themselves or thinks out loud, the assistant keeps listening and responding, which is noisy and distracting.

Suggestion for Grok / voice-assistant developers:
- Add a natural-language pause: user says "hold on a second" or "wait" and the assistant goes silent until the wake word is spoken again.
- No button press required — just a spoken phrase that suspends listening.
- Resume on the next wake-word utterance.
- This matches how people actually talk: they narrate, they think aloud, they need the assistant to shut up without a gesture.

Why it matters:
- ADHD and similar conditions make it hard to manage attention and interruptions.
- A pause feature reduces cognitive load — the user doesn't have to fight the assistant to think.
- Most other voice assistants have some version of this; Grok does not yet.

Status: feature request / developer note. Worth submitting to Grok feedback.

* Email client friction (Gmail)
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: gmail-ux
:END:
Problem: When actively corresponding with someone (e.g. Lulie), the active thread does not stay pinned at the top of the inbox. User has to dig through sent mail or search to find the latest reply.

Suggestion:
- Pin or auto-surface the most recent thread in an active conversation to the top of the list.
- Or a "focus mode" that keeps the current correspondence visible.

Status: noted as UX friction. Not a code task for the foundation repo, but relevant to the overall tooling experience.
